Family navigation guide

Open the public link or scan the QR. No account, installation or headset is required. Select English or Arabic, then Start Your Journey for a guided tour or Explore Freely for self-directed navigation. Family Challenge has five collaborative questions.

DeviceMove / look / interact
ComputerW / Up: forward; S / Down: back; A / Left and D / Right: strafe. Drag mouse to look; click an exhibit or aim and press E. M opens map; Escape pauses.
Phone or tabletHold the on-screen direction buttons to walk. Swipe the scene to look around. Tap glowing objects and signs. Use the Next, Back and Map buttons to travel.
Compatible VR headsetOpen in a WebXR-enabled headset browser and enter VR. Point the controller ray at an exhibit and select. Select the ground to teleport. Left thumbstick moves; right thumbstick turns in 30-degree increments. Exit VR using headset controls or the Exit VR button in browser mode.

Use Accessibility to reduce motion, lower graphics quality or open the illustrated low-bandwidth mode. Audio narration has on-screen story captions. Never use a headset in a crowded area, supervise children, move slowly, stop if uncomfortable, and follow the headset manufacturer's age and safety recommendations.

Teacher instruction guide

Suggested duration: 25–40 minutes; independent or paired exploration. Learning intentions: identify the events that established the UAE, describe all seven emirates, distinguish heritage from modern achievements and imaginative future concepts, and reflect on cooperation and responsible innovation.

  1. Introduce the theme: Seven Emirates. One Nation. One Dream.
  2. Model walking, looking and selecting an object. Ask learners to visit the heritage village and collect five discoveries.
  3. Visit the founding exhibition, Union House interpretation and symbolic map activity. Compare 1968, 2 December 1971 and 10 February 1972.
  4. Ask groups to explore emirate portals and match landmarks to emirates. Complete the modern achievements and future worlds.
  5. Complete each world's mission for a passport stamp. Use family prompts for discussion and the future design task for a creative response.
  6. Celebrate the final world and download the personalized certificate after earning all seven stars.

Assessment: students can explain one historical milestone, name the seven emirates and share a responsible technology idea. Check the local passport or a student-provided screenshot; no public leaderboard exists.

Historical context & scope

The interactive landscapes and monuments are original stylized educational reconstructions rather than verified photographs or exact replicas. The future world is imaginative. Noor provides a bilingual curated, rule-based interactive guide, not an unrestricted generative AI chatbot. Arabic narration uses a natural-sounding AI-generated voice with pre-recorded audio for all main worlds, exhibits and missions. Adjust the speed to suit each learner. English narration uses available browser voices.

Privacy and accessibility

Progress and optional creative responses stay in the browser's local storage. Certificate names are used locally to create an image. Do not enter personal information in the pride wall. The experience is public by link; there are no visitor accounts, paid subscriptions or public rankings.

WebXR support depends on headset hardware, browser, HTTPS and device permissions; a physical headset is needed for hardware acceptance testing. The desktop browser fallback uses a simplified software-rendered model; an illustrated mode is also available for low bandwidth.
